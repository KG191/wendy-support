Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 October 2026

Wendy requires no account. Saved conversations stay on your device. Coaching text and speech requests are processed by our secure service and the providers described below.

Coaching and relevant conversation memory

Your current messages and conversation context are sent through Wendy's Cloudflare-hosted service to OpenAI or Anthropic to generate coaching responses. When “Remember relevant conversations” is enabled, the app selects relevant excerpts of your earlier saved user messages locally and includes them in the coaching request. This does not upload your entire saved archive. Turn this feature off in Settings to stop including earlier sessions.

Wendy's voice

When automatic reading is enabled or you tap Read, Wendy's response text and adjacent passage context are sent through our service to ElevenLabs to generate audio. You can turn off automatic reading and use text only. If you use keyboard dictation, Apple's keyboard and speech settings control that input.

Local storage and your choices

Saved sessions and preferences are stored on your device. Delete individual Saved Sessions, or use Settings > Clear Conversation History to delete current and saved conversations. Deleted sessions are excluded from future memory. Deleting local content does not recall requests already processed by service providers. Device backup settings may affect copies held in your backups.

Security and service operation

Requests are encrypted in transit. Provider credentials remain on the server. Apple App Attest verifies app installations. Our service stores installation verification records, short-lived challenges and usage counters; daily hashed IP identifiers help prevent abuse. It does not keep a conversation archive. Operational error records exclude conversation text and credentials.

Providers and retention

Providers process requests under their applicable service terms, account settings and retention policies; processing does not mean data is never retained. See OpenAI, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Cloudflare and Apple. Avoid entering unnecessary personal or confidential workplace information.

Subscriptions and advertising

Apple processes subscription payments. Wendy uses purchase status to enable paid features and does not receive your payment-card details. Wendy does not sell conversation data or use it for advertising tracking.

Age and contact

Wendy is intended for adults aged 18 and over. For privacy enquiries contact wendyproperformancecoach@gmail.com. Material changes will be reflected in this policy's effective date.
